01 / A NOTE FROM MY HEART

Happy Birthday,
Meri Sabse Khaas Insaan

Pata hai, aaj ka din mere liye kitna khaas hai? Kyunki aaj us insaan ka janamdin hai jise sochte hi mere chehre par muskurahat aa jaati hai. Tum meri zindagi ka wo hissa ho jise main lafzon mein poori tarah bayan nahi kar sakta. Tumhari ek chhoti si baat mera din bana deti hai, aur tumhari ek muskurahat mere dil ko sukoon de deti hai.

Shayad tumhe andaza bhi nahi hoga ki tum meri zindagi mein kitni ahmiyat rakhti ho. Aaj tumhare birthday par main bas itna kehna chahta hoon ki tum meri har dua mein shamil ho. Main dil se chahta hoon ki tumhari zindagi mein kabhi kisi cheez ki kami na ho. Tumhare har sapne poore ho, har manzil tumhe mile aur tum hamesha khush raho. Tumhare saath baat karna, tumhari fikr karna aur tumhe khush dekhna mujhe sach mein bahut pasand hai.

Din kitna bhi busy ho, ek baar tumhara khayal zaroor aa hi jaata hai. Aaj ke din main Allah se bas yehi dua karta hoon ki tumhari zindagi hamesha khushiyon se bhari rahe. Tumhare chehre ki muskurahat kabhi kam na ho aur tum jis cheez ko dil se chaho, wo tumhe zaroor mile.

Aur haan, thank you... meri zindagi mein aane ke liye, meri baaton ko sunne ke liye, aur bina kuch kahe phir mujhe samajhne ke liye. Tum sach mein mere liye bahut special ho.

Janamdin mubarak ho Kutiyaa. Khush raho, muskurate raho aur hamesha mere dil ke sabse kareeb raho. ❤️
